B by kind (final run): spread 1.0, pronouns 0.83, implied 0.88, accepted offers 1.0, vague dates
1.0, updates 1.0, restatements 1.0, contradictions 1.0, opinions 1.0, German / Spanish / Arabic /
Hindi 0.88. A: 0.14, 0, 0.25, 0, 0.17, 0.17, 0.71, 0.33, 0.6, 0. Duplicates count an expected fact
held by more than one memory by its words, so two distinct facts naming the same thing
("Zoe started kindergarten", "Zoe loves the art corner") count too; the rules' own restatements
("My favourite cuisine is Thai" / "Thai food is my favourite") are in every arm.
