# What the field does that we do not, and what is worth borrowing

Written 2026-09-23 from a seven-lens research pass (ingest synthesis, temporal, graph, fusion,
answerer-side rendering, evaluation rulers, multilingual) over the 2024–2026 literature and this
repository, each lens followed by an adversarial verification. Every technique is mapped to our
code as **have / partial / missing / planned / killed**, every gain carries the measurement it
rests on or the word UNMEASURED, and every borrow has a gate. The constraints are fixed: **no LLM
call at query time**, retrieval p99 < 300 ms on 8 vCPU, multilingual, no cross-encoder.

Companion documents: [ROADMAP-2026-09.md](ROADMAP-2026-09.md) is the plan of record,
[HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) the state of execution.

## The failure shape we are fixing

From `benchmark/results/locomo_judged_v5.json` (HEAD, 304 questions, 233 answerable, strict
0.833 / lenient 0.893 / refined 0.631): **evidence recall is 0.991 and `evidence_all_recall` is
also 0.991** — every gold turn is in the bundle for all but one wrong answer. Retrieval is not the
constraint. The 39 wrong answerable answers classify as:

| Class | v2 | v5 | What fixes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abstained or hedged with the gold present | 26 | **13** | the answer protocol (already half-fixed; the standard-ruler answer prompt finishes it) |
| Partial list — correct under the lenient ruler, incomplete under strict | 13 | **14** | precomputed dated aggregates (enumeration answered from one line, not reassembled from 100) |
| Wrong salience — a plausible non-gold entry chosen | 22 | **11** | rank-anchored rendering (lost-in-the-middle), less noise per line |
| Judge granularity, date arithmetic, empty answers | 14 | **1** | fixed between v2 and v5 |

So the remaining work is **what is stored and how it is rendered**, not what is found. The three
biggest published systems agree: Mnemis, EverMemOS and Hindsight all precompute synthesis units
with an LLM at ingest and keep the verbatim text alongside.

## Ranked borrow list

Ordered by (strength of evidence × expected gain) ÷ cost, zero-query-cost items first.

1. **Stop stamping the date and speaker into stored content** — fixes the doubled render *and* the
   start-anchored ingest rules in one change. Gate: rule facts per turn rise from 0.36; rendered
   chars fall; v6 strict ≥ v5. *In flight.*
2. **Rank-anchored rendering block** above the chronological timeline. Gate: v6 by-category vs v5,
   bundle tokens not materially larger. *In flight.*
3. **Graph seeding, tie-break and fact text.** Gate: graph facts stop duplicating turns; the
   with/without-graph judged ablation (Phase 4 step 6) becomes meaningful. *In flight.*
4. **The field's ruler**: Mem0's verbatim ACCURACY_PROMPT as a named ruler, Mem0's verbatim answer
   prompt for the comparable column, category 5 separate, judge model recorded per file. Gate: the
   standard column exists and is reproducible. Worth up to +6 pp with no system change.
5. **Dated per-(subject, predicate) aggregates** from the dormant `BeliefService`, rendered as one
   enumerable line. Gate: strict multi-hop ≥ +3, `false_merge == 0.0`.
6. **Paraphrase dedup** (compare LLM-authored candidates against their top-20 vector neighbours,
   not behind a Jaccard gate) — a precondition for any LLM-at-ingest work.
7. **Whole-session extraction** with turn citations and a zero-LLM grounding filter (Phase 4 step 8).
   Gate: +3 strict, p50 unchanged, `false_merge == 0.0`, no duplicate facts across outbox retries.
8. **Weighted RRF or DBSF**, fitted offline from dumped per-leg gold ranks, spent on halving depth.
   Gate: `evidence_all_recall ≥ 0.99` at the lower depth.
9. **Entity → memory routing** as one more RRF list once entities are in the payload.
10. **Event-time vs mention-time**, weekday rendering, `dateparser` resolution (Phase 4 step 4).
11. **Multilingual correctness set**: verbatim retention for non-Latin scripts, NFKC + casefold,
    per-language negation, script-aware token estimation, cross-lingual and code-switched gate rows.
12. **doc2query hypothetical questions** at ingest, filtered — measure or drop.

Instrumentation that must come first (benchmark-only, zero runtime cost): dump per-item
`[record_id, kind, retrievers, fused_rank]` and the gold turns' rank in the dense-only, sparse-only
and fused lists. Today a bundle is stored as four integers, so no fusion weight, depth or salience
decision can be evidenced. A failure taxonomy of the 39 misses is already possible offline from the
v5 records plus `benchmark/data/locomo10.json`.

## Do not

- **Mnemis System-2** hierarchical LLM browsing: +4.2 points for ~2.4 s of LLM per query (3,637 s
  over 1,540 questions). Its zero-LLM System-1 scores 89.1 and still carries an 8B reranker.
- **EverMemOS's sufficiency re-query** (second retrieval round on 31 % of questions), **Letta's
  agentic file search**, **MemoryOS's 4.9 LLM calls per response**, **Mastra's 30k-token log**.
- **Any cross-encoder** (measured here: −5.2 nDCG on SciFact, 21× latency) and **Qwen3
  embedders/rerankers on CPU** (2.2 s and 35.8 s measured on this class of box).
- **Late interaction, query multi-vector, Qdrant Discovery**: an extra encoder pass or no headroom.
- **Recency decay in ranking**: LoCoMo asks about old events; temporal is already 0.95.
- **Claiming any number against a published one until the standard column exists.** The Mnemis
  harness scores Mem0 at 66.3 where Mem0 self-reports 92.5 — a 26-point swing from the ruler alone.
